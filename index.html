Ember & Saffron is a love letter to the flavours that raised us — rewritten for the way we eat now.

We travel through Pakistan by way of our pantry: the smoky chillies of Multan, the fragrant rice of Punjab, black lime from the coast. Familiar ingredients find new rhythm over live fire, with a lightness that lets every story speak.
